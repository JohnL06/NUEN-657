#concept #incident
The September 11, 2001 attacks — the incident that exposed how disorganized multi-agency response was, and drove creation of the [[NIMS]].

- Washington and New York were handled as separate incidents
- [[HPSD-5]] (2003) then directed development of NIMS, adopted across all federal agencies
