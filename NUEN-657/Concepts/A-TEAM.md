#concept #asset
Advisory Team for Environment, Food, and Health — the interagency group that turns [[FRMAC]] data into recommendations.

- Core members: [[EPA]], [[FDA]], [[CDC]], and [[USDA]]
- Provides recommendations and guidance on protective action guides, interpretation of regulations, and modeling assumptions
- Exists because FRMAC itself does NOT issue recommendations — FRMAC measures and assesses, the A-Team advises

Related: [[FRMAC]], [[EPA]], [[FDA]], [[CDC]], [[USDA]], [[EPA400]]
