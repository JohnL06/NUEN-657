#concept #datasource
Web-based platform for managing chemical, biological, radiological, and nuclear response data, listed in lecture under [[FEMA]] logistics.

- Per course notes, now offered commercially as MissionEdge and usable for training purposes, with a simulation component for ground deposition
- Sits alongside RadResponder as the data-sharing side of a response, as distinct from modeling tools like [[RASCAL]]
