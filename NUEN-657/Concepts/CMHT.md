#concept #asset
Consequence Management Home Team — the [[DOE]] reach-back advisory group that supports deployed teams from a fixed location rather than the field.

- Sits in a room in Las Vegas or Sandia, or works over a bridge line between them
- Acts in an advisory role to the people in the field
- Starts activating other assets — it is what would activate the [[NARAC]]
- The bridge line is open: people can call in and the whole team hears what is being said
- [[REACTS]] may be part of the home team, especially at the very beginning
- First of the three initial [[FRMAC]] elements: scalable, near-immediate technical support, ahead of the [[CMAC]] and [[CMRT]]
- Receives field data by email, fax, or call-in on the bridge line when [[RadResponder]] is not used
