#concept #asset
Civil Support Teams — National Guard / state assets that support civil authorities at a suspected CBRN incident.

- Full-time WMD-CST units, with at least one in every state
- Detect and identify unknown CBRN material, assess consequences, and advise responders
- Often the fastest-arriving asset with identification capability, ahead of federal teams like [[RAP]]
- Every state has one, usually in the state capital (California has one for the north and one for the south)
- Regional and state focused
- State controlled — the governor and the adjutant general, not a federal chain
- "Same" equipment, instrumentation, and vehicles across teams

Related: [[DOD]], [[RAP]], [[SLTT]], [[NRIA]]
