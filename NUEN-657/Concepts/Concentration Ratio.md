#concept #transport
How much of a radionuclide is in vegetation compared with the soil it grows in.

- CR = (veg) / (soil)
- A function of soil type, radionuclide, and plant species
- Typical values given in lecture: Pu ~1e-4 (up to 1e-1 at Rocky Flats); Cs-137 ~7
- General ordering: Sr >> I >> Ba > Cs, and Ru > Ce > Y > Pm > Zr > Nb > Pu
- Elements plants need are taken up readily, which is why [[Sr-90]] (calcium's [[Congener]]) ranks so high; some elements (iodine, cobalt, uranium, radium) are found in plants with no biological purpose
