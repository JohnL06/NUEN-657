#concept #transport
A chemically similar element that a radionuclide tends to follow through the environment and the body — knowing the congener's behavior lets you predict the radionuclide's.

- Sr → Ca: [[Sr-90]] behaves like calcium and seeks bone
- Cs → K: [[Cs-137]] behaves like potassium and deposits largely in muscle
- The tracking is imperfect and its strength varies — Sr:Ca is more constant in biological systems than Cs:K
- The basis of the [[Observed Ratio]]
