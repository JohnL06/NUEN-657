#concept #nuclide
Cesium-137 — fission product with a 30 year half-life; one of the three most studied radionuclides.

- Bound by clays in soil, so it tends to stay near the surface
- [[Congener]] is potassium, though Cs:K is not as constant in biological systems as Sr:Ca
- About 80% deposits in muscle
- [[Effective half-life]] depends on age, sex, and diet: infants 19 ± 8 days, adult men ~105 days, women turn over faster than men
- Diet dominates: Laplanders carried ~50× more Cs than other Finns from eating reindeer
- Cs-134 (2.06 yr) appears alongside it in reactor releases; also a candidate [[RDD]] source
