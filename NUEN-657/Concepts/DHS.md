#concept #agency
Department of Homeland Security — the department [[FEMA]] sits under, and the hub for national-level incident coordination.

- Runs the [[NOC]], which the [[LFA]] notifies once it has been determined
- [[HPSD-5]] directed the development of [[NIMS]] under DHS
- Under the [[NRIA]], Cooperating Agencies provide technical and resource support to DHS and the coordinating agencies
