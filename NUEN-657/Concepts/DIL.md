#concept #data
Derived Intervention Level — "concentration derived from the intervention level of dose at which introduction of protective measures should be considered." (FDA 1998, via EPA-400 glossary)

- The food/liquid version of a [[DRL]]: a measurable concentration that, if eaten, could meet a [[PAG]]
- Hit it and the food is embargoed — you are intervening between the food and the people
- Set by [[FDA]]; the 1998 FDA food guidance was integrated into the current EPA-400

Related: [[DRL]], [[PAG]], [[FDA]], [[USDA]], [[Foliar Deposition]]
