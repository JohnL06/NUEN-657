#concept #agency
Department of Energy — owns much of the US nuclear complex and provides the bulk of dedicated federal radiological response capability.

- Manages and organizes the [[FRMAC]]: whatever agency holds [[Primary Authority]], it is DOE that runs radiological monitoring on the ground and in the air
- Fields the [[NEST]] programs: [[RAP]], [[CMRT]], [[AMS]], [[REACTS]], [[NARC]]
- Relinquishes FRMAC control to [[EPA]] only after the emergency is stabilized, offsite releases have stopped, offsite conditions have been evaluated, an initial long-range monitoring plan exists with [[SLTT]] and other federal agencies, and EPA has assurance of federal support
- Would itself be the [[LFA]] for incidents at facilities it owns or authorizes
- Its emergency response assets are run through the [[NNSA]], DOE's semi-autonomous nuclear security agency
