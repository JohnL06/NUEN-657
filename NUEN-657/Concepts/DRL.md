#concept #data
Derived Response Level — "a level of radioactivity in an environmental medium that would be expected to produce a dose equal to its corresponding Protective Action Guide." (EPA-400 glossary)

- The measurable stand-in for a [[PAG]] that [[Field Teams]] can actually check
- Lecture example: tell teams to report when they hit 5 mrem/h, because 5 mrem/h over ~100 h reaches the 1 rem evacuation PAG
- Calculated with [[FRMAC manual]] methods; EPA-400 Table 4-3 gives default drinking water DRLs

Related: [[PAG]], [[DIL]], [[Field Teams]], [[FRMAC manual]]
