#concept  #data
The quantity of radioactive material that has settled out of the plume onto the ground, structures, water, or vegetation (surface activity, e.g., Bq/m²).

- Drives ground-shine dose and longer-term contamination of soil and crops — central to [[relocation]] and re-entry decisions
- Time of year matters: during the growing season, deposited contaminants can be taken up into plants; close to harvest time this is less of a concern
- Can later become airborne again via [[Resuspension]]
- Measured directly by [[Field Teams]] (soil/water samples, crop samples) and by automated environmental monitors

Related: [[Air concentration]], [[Resuspension]], [[Field Teams]], [[relocation]]
