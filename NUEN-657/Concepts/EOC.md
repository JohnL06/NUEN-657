#concept #center
Emergency Operations Center — "the physical location where the coordination of information and resources to support incident management (on-scene operations) activities normally takes place." It may be a temporary facility or a permanently established one, perhaps at a higher level of organization within a jurisdiction. (IS-100.c glossary)

- [[FRMAC]] sends each EOC 1 liaison and/or 1 scientific advisor, traveling with a laptop, aircard, phone, and reference materials
- It is understood that seating at the EOC may be limited

Related: [[FRMAC]], [[ICS]], [[JFO]]
