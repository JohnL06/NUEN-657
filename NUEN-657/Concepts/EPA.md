#concept #agency
Environmental Protection Agency — sets protective action guidance and takes over long-term radiological monitoring.

- Publishes [[EPA400]], the PAG manual behind [[evacuation]], [[shelter in place]], and [[relocation]] decisions
- Assumes control of [[FRMAC]] from [[DOE]] once the emergency is stabilized and the transition conditions are met
- Fields the National Decontamination Team, including the [[RERT]]
- Has several specialized teams that provide aid during a radiological emergency: the [[RERT]] (drawing on [[RADNET]]) and [[CBRN CMAD]] (operating [[ASPECT]])

Related: [[EPA400]], [[FRMAC]], [[DOE]], [[RERT]], [[RADNET]], [[CBRN CMAD]], [[ASPECT]]
