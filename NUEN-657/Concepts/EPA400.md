#concept #guidance
EPA-400 — the "Manual of Protective Action Guides and Protective Actions for Nuclear Incidents," the primary US guidance document for Protective Action Guides (PAGs).

- Establishes the dose-based triggers used to decide among [[evacuation]], [[shelter in place]], and [[relocation]]
- Used to reduce the risk of stochastic effects (primarily cancer and severe hereditary effects) once acute deterministic-effect thresholds ([[emergency limits]]) are under control
- Guidance rather than binding law — "there are no laws, only guidelines," which gives responders and decision makers flexibility (and is why contractors advising a decision maker can't simply dictate what to do)
- Current version is 2017 (EPA-400/R-17/001), "PAG Manual: Protective Action Guides and Planning Guidance for Radiological Incidents"; the original was EPA 1992, revised 2013 and 2017 — see [[EPA_PAG_Manual_2017.pdf|the 2017 manual]]
- Legal basis: 44 CFR 351 and Executive Order 12656
- Dose projections moved from ICRP 26/30 (1992) to ICRP 60
- Scope widened from NPP fission products to fuel cycle and weapons facilities, transport, radiopharmaceuticals, launch vehicles, [[RDD]]s, and INDs
- PAGs are implemented with [[FRMAC manual]] methods, and the manual tells readers to use the current FRMAC Assessment Manual
- Changes from 1992: FDA 1998 food guidance integrated, KI PAG lowered to 5 rem, the 5 rem / 50 year relocation PAG removed
- Organized into three [[Response Phases]]; PAG values summarized in [[PAG]]
