#concept #guidance
EPA-400 — the "Manual of Protective Action Guides and Protective Actions for Nuclear Incidents," the primary US guidance document for Protective Action Guides (PAGs).

- Establishes the dose-based triggers used to decide among [[evacuation]], [[shelter in place]], and [[relocation]]
- Used to reduce the risk of stochastic effects (primarily cancer and severe hereditary effects) once acute deterministic-effect thresholds ([[emergency limits]]) are under control
- Guidance rather than binding law — "there are no laws, only guidelines," which gives responders and decision makers flexibility (and is why contractors advising a decision maker can't simply dictate what to do)

Related: [[emergency limits]], [[FRMAC manual]], [[evacuation]], [[relocation]], [[shelter in place]]
