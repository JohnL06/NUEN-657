#concept #data
The combined rate at which a radionuclide leaves the body through both radioactive decay and biological elimination: 1/T_eff = 1/T_phys + 1/T_bio.

- Always shorter than either the physical or the biological half-life alone
- Varies with age, sex, and diet — for [[Cs-137]], infants 19 ± 8 days versus ~105 days for adult men
- About 7.6 days for iodine in the thyroid ([[I-131]])

Related: [[Cs-137]], [[I-131]], [[Sr-90]]
