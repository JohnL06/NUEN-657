#concept #asset
Fly Away Laboratory — FRMAC's deployable on-site radioanalytical lab.

- Supports health and safety measurements, sample screening and prioritization, and only LIMITED sample analysis
- Analytical capabilities: gamma spectroscopy and gross alpha/beta counting; NO radiochemistry
- Gamma spectroscopy accepts any sample size or geometry; gross α/β counting takes air filters, swipes, and (limited) soil
- Anything needing radiochemistry goes to fixed off-site labs
