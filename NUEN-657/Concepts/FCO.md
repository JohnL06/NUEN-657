#concept #role
Federal Coordinating Officer — the official designated to coordinate federal assistance in the affected area once a major disaster declaration is issued.

- Designated as part of a [[Stafford Act]] response, alongside establishment of a [[JFO]] and availability of all [[ESF]]s
- Distinct from the [[LFA]], which holds [[Primary Authority]] for the N/R incident itself

Related: [[Stafford Act]], [[JFO]], [[LFA]], [[ESF]]
