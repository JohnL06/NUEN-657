Food and Drug Administration — the Health and Human Services component governing medical countermeasures and contaminated food.

- Covers use of [[Potassium Iodide|potassium iodide]] (thyroid blocking) and Prussian blue (cesium decorporation)
- Also the authority behind limits on contaminated agricultural and food products
