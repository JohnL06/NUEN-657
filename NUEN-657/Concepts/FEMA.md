Federal Emergency Management Agency.

- Federal emergency management existed in some form since ~1803; between 1803 and 1979 the approach was piecemeal, spread across multiple agencies with disjointed plans
- 1979: FEMA created as the first comprehensive federal emergency response plan, absorbing emergency response duties from those multiple agencies
- 1988: FEMA's Director authorized to prepare the [[FRP]]
- 1996: jointly developed the [[FRERP]] with the NRC for peacetime radiological emergencies
- Per course housekeeping notes, FEMA has reportedly been significantly scaled back under the current administration

Related: [[FRP]], [[FRERP]], [[NRF]], [[NIMS]]
