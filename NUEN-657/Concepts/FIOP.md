#concept #policy
Federal Interagency Operational Plan (referred to in lecture as the "Response and Recovery Federal Interagency Operational Manual").

- Now houses the incident annexes that used to be called "Incident Annexes" under the [[NRF]]
- Addresses the unique aspects of responding to seven broad incident categories (e.g., biological, nuclear/radiological, cyber, mass evacuation)
- Its [[NRIA]] covers the nuclear/radiological incident category specifically

Related: [[NRF]], [[ESF]], [[NRIA]]
