Federal Radiological Emergency Response Plan — signed 1996.

- Interagency document: the NRC and FEMA jointly developed the government response for peacetime radiological emergencies within the US
- Scope: nuclear installation incidents, transportation incidents, nuclear weapon incidents, earth-bound return satellites, and terrorist attacks
- Predecessor radiological-specific framework, later folded into the all-hazards [[NRF]]
