#concept #guidance
Guidance manual of the Federal Radiological Monitoring and Assessment Center (FRMAC) — DOE's field organization that coordinates environmental monitoring, sampling, and dose assessment during a radiological emergency.

- Used alongside [[EPA400]] for dose assessment and to help reduce the risk of stochastic health effects after the acute (deterministic-effect) phase is addressed
- Listed in the course as one of the manuals used for dose assessment (DOE FRMAC)

Related: [[FRMAC]], [[EPA400]], [[emergency limits]]
