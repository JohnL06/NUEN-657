#concept 
Federal Response Plan — authorized in 1988, when FEMA's Director was directed to prepare it.

- Established a system of federal assistance to state and local governments
- Required all states to prepare individual Emergency Operations Plans
- Augmented in 1992 by the 1968 [[NCP]] (expanded via [[Environmental Response, Compensation and Liability Act]])
- Eventually superseded by the [[FRERP]] (for radiological emergencies specifically) and later folded into the all-hazards [[NRF]]

Related: [[FEMA]], [[NCP]], [[FRERP]], [[NRF]]
