 #datasource #concept 
"Boots on the ground" personnel who take direct measurements to confirm and refine model predictions during a radiological response.

Equipment and tasks:
- Air samples (LV & HV pumps, filters)
- Soil / water samples (scoops, bags, bottles)
- Beta probes
- Alpha probes — detecting alphas suggests fuel itself is being released/burned
- GM/gamma detectors (dose rate)
- In-situ / HPGe spectroscopy
- GPS
- Data recording: paper, laptop, tablet PC, toughbook
- Crop samples

Work alongside a dedicated lab facility with known Minimum Detectable Activities (MDAs) and throughput for low-level sample analysis.
