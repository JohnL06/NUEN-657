#concept #transport
Direct contamination of plants by radioactive material landing on their surfaces.

- Can pass straight to grazing animals or humans by ingestion, or be absorbed metabolically from the plant surface
- For short-lived radionuclides the soil pathway is unimportant — this is the pathway that matters
- Seasonal: direct contamination is the main concern in harvest season and lowest in winter; deposition on old stems and surface roots can be stored until it is absorbed in spring
- Plant structure matters — wheat entraps fallout particles, so cereals see high foliar concentrations
- Removed over time by [[Weathering]]
