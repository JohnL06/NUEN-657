#concept #nuclide
Iodine-131 — fission product with an 8 day half-life; one of the three most studied radionuclides and the main early-phase thyroid dose driver.

- Iodine is essential for normal thyroid function, so the body concentrates it there — about 0.3 of intake reaches the thyroid, with a 7.6 day [[Effective half-life]]
- The short half-life makes uptake from soil unimportant; the concern is [[Foliar Deposition]]
- Grass-cow-infant pathway: iodine deposited on pasture is eaten by cows and released in milk
- Semi-volatile, with a [[Weathering]] half-time of about 10 days on vegetation
- Taking stable iodine ([[Potassium Iodide]]) as late as about 2 hours after exposure still reduces the dose
- Arrives with other fission iodines (I-132, I-133) and their parent Te-132
