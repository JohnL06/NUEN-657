#concept #system
Incident Command System — "a standardized approach to the command, control, and coordination of on-scene incident management, providing a common hierarchy within which personnel from multiple organizations can be effective... the combination of procedures, personnel, facilities, equipment, and communications operating within a common organizational structure." (IS-100.c glossary)

- Standardized, on-scene, all-hazards approach; scales up or down to match incident complexity while respecting agency/jurisdictional authority
- One of the three major components of [[NIMS]] (under Command and Coordination) — ICS is only one part of NIMS
- Built on 14 NIMS management characteristics: Common Terminology, Modular Organization, Management by Objectives, Incident Action Planning, Manageable Span of Control, Incident Facilities and Locations, Comprehensive Resource Management, Integrated Communications, Establishment and Transfer of Command, Unified Command, Chain of Command and Unity of Command, Accountability, Dispatch/Deployment, Information and Intelligence Management
- Always has an [[Incident Commander]] — or a Unified Command when more than one agency has jurisdiction or an incident crosses political boundaries
