#concept #soil
The swapping of cations dissolved in soil water with cations sorbed onto the surfaces of soil particles, mostly clays.

- Most nutrient ions are not dissolved in water but held on particle surfaces — a far larger reservoir than solution alone could hold
- Without clays binding elements, nutrients (and radionuclides) would be rapidly leached
- Surface area is enormous: about 1 m3 of loam is estimated at roughly 2 million m2
- Cations are generally sorbed more strongly than anions, since soil carries more negative charge
- Quantified for transport purposes by [[Kd]]

Related: [[Soil texture]], [[Kd]], [[Leaching]]
