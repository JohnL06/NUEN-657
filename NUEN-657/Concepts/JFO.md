#concept #center
Joint Field Office — the temporary federal multi-agency coordination center established in or near the affected area during a declared disaster.

- Established as part of a [[Stafford Act]] response, along with designation of a [[FCO]] and availability of all [[ESF]]s
- A coordination facility, not an on-scene command post

Related: [[Stafford Act]], [[FCO]], [[ESF]], [[JOC]]
