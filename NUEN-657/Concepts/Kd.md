#concept #transport
Distribution coefficient — the ratio of a radionuclide's concentration on the solid phase to its concentration in the liquid phase, describing how it partitions between soil and water.

- Kd = (solid phase) / (liquid phase), in mL/g
- High Kd — little leaching, little movement; low Kd — more leaching, fast movement
- If a radionuclide sits mainly in the liquid phase, it moves
- Smaller multivalent ions usually show larger Kd than univalent ions
- Depends on both the radionuclide and the soil — the key number for whether contamination stays put
- Matters for plants too: for high-Kd radionuclides, or ones plants discriminate against, soil ingestion dominates over root uptake
