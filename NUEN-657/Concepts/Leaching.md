#concept #transport
Downward removal of radionuclides through soil, carried by moving water.

- Moves contamination from the root zone into deeper soil
- As water moves, different ionic species sorb at different rates: the most weakly bound species are at the leading edge, and cations are generally held back more than anions
- Governed by [[Kd]] — low Kd means more leaching and fast movement, high Kd means little

Related: [[Kd]], [[Percolation]], [[Ion Exchange]]
