#concept #transport
The mass of soil suspended in a volume of air — used to estimate airborne activity from surface soil contamination, since some particles are always in the air.

- X (Bq/m3) = M (g of soil per m3 of air) × activity concentration of the surface soil (Bq/g)
- M ranges from about 10^-6 in pristine areas to 10^-2 in windy, dry areas
- One of two ways to estimate resuspended [[Air concentration]]; the other is the [[Resuspension factor]]

Related: [[Resuspension]], [[Resuspension factor]], [[Air concentration]]
