#concept #asset
National Atmospheric Release Advisory Center — [[DOE]]'s plume prediction center at Lawrence Livermore, CA, and the federal modeling resource activated for a release.

- Activated by the [[CMHT]] as the response spins up
- A scientist sleeps with the laptop at home; simulations can start within 15 minutes
- Automated real-time 3-D plume model predictions for nuclear, radiological, chemical or biological releases, available in minutes via an internet/web tool
- Standalone simple plume modeling tools run on an end-user's computer with no connection to NARAC
- 24x7 scientific and technical support on uninterruptible, backed-up power
- Draws on worldwide weather and geographic data: observed and forecast weather, terrain and land surface, maps, population
- Live feedback between field measurements and the model, so predictions are corrected against what [[Field Teams]] actually measure
