#concept #policy
National Contingency Plan — originally established in 1968 for oil spill response.

- Scope expanded by the 1980 [[Environmental Response, Compensation and Liability Act]] (CERCLA/"Superfund") to include emergency hazardous waste removal and facility-specific contingency plans
- Used in 1992 to augment the [[FRP]]

Related: [[Environmental Response, Compensation and Liability Act]], [[FRP]]
