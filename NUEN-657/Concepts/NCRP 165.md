#concept #guidance
NCRP Report No. 165, "Responding to a Radiological or Nuclear Terrorism Incident: A Guide for Decision Makers" (2010).

- Source of the 50 rad (0.5 Gy) decision dose — decide whether to remove a responder or continue the mission, based on operational awareness and mission priorities
- Source of the dangerous-radiation zone (≥10 R/h) — restrict actions to time-sensitive, mission-critical work such as lifesaving
- Cited in EPA-400 for "time-sensitive mission critical" response worker activities
