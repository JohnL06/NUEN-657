#concept #agency
National Nuclear Security Administration — the semi-autonomous agency within [[DOE]] responsible for nuclear security, and the home of DOE's radiological emergency response assets.

- Runs the [[NEST]] programs — [[RAP]], [[CMRT]], [[CMHT]], [[AMS]], [[NARAC]], [[REACTS]] — and manages the [[FRMAC]]
- Its Consequence Management program leads the federal monitoring and assessment side of a response
- AMS aircraft operate out of the Nevada National Security Site's Remote Sensing Laboratory (Nellis AFB and Joint Base Andrews)

Related: [[DOE]], [[NEST]], [[FRMAC]], [[CMHT]], [[AMS]]
