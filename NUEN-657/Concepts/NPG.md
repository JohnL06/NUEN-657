#concept #policy
National Preparedness Goal — doctrine describing "a secure and resilient nation with the capabilities required across the whole community to prevent, protect against, mitigate, respond to, and recover from the threats and hazards that pose the greatest risk."

- Organized around 5 mission areas: Prevention, Protection, Mitigation, Response, Recovery
- Supported by 32 core capabilities
- The [[NRF]] sets the strategy/doctrine for delivering the response core capabilities identified in the NPG
