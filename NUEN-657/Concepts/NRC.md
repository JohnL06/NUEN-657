#concept #agency
Nuclear Regulatory Commission — the independent federal agency that licenses and regulates civilian use of nuclear materials and reactors in the US.

- Jointly developed the [[FRERP]] with FEMA (1996) for peacetime radiological emergencies
- Maintains/distributes response tools such as [[RASCAL]] for reactor-specific atmospheric and dose simulations
- A key federal partner in the [[NRIA]] response structure

Related: [[FRERP]], [[RASCAL]], [[NRIA]], [[FEMA]]
