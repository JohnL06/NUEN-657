#concept 
National Response Framework — 2008 (revised 2013, 2019). Part of the National Strategy for Homeland Security, built on [[NIMS]] and [[ICS]].

- Sets guiding principles for how all levels of domestic response prepare for and deliver a unified national response to disasters and emergencies (from acts of God to terrorist activities), with annexes for specific event types (biological, chemical, nuclear/radiological, etc.)
- Superseded the [[NRP]] (2004)

**NRF response vision**: sets the strategy and doctrine for how the whole community builds, sustains, and delivers the response core capabilities identified in the [[NPG]], across 5 mission areas (Prevention, Protection, Mitigation, Response, Recovery) and 32 core capabilities.

**The document is comprised of:**
- Core Document (51 pages)
- [[ESF]] Annexes — federal coordinating structures grouping resources/capabilities into functional areas most frequently needed in a national response
- Support Annexes — mechanisms for private-sector/NGO/federal-partner support; superseded as underlying legislation changes
- Formerly "Incident Annexes," now housed under the [[FIOP]] incident annexes, covering seven broad incident categories (e.g., biological, nuclear/radiological, cyber, mass evacuation)
- Two other annexes have been removed and are now under [[ESF 14]]
