#concept #role
Federal Onsite Commander — per lecture, the federal official who determines that no federal assistance is required and then acts as liaison for the response.

- One branch of the federal support determination: no federal assistance with an OSC acting as liaison, versus a full [[Unified Command]] response
- Closely related to the "On-Scene Coordinator" role used under the [[NCP]] for oil and hazardous substance response

Related: [[Unified Command]], [[NRIA]], [[NCP]]
