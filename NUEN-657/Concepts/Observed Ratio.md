#concept #transport
Compares a radionuclide-to-[[Congener]] ratio in a sample against the same ratio in its precursor one step back along the food chain.

- OR(sample-precursor) = (radionuclide / congener)sample ÷ (radionuclide / congener)precursor
- Example: the Ca-Sr OR is about 0.1, so if forage carries 10 Bq Sr per g Ca, milk would carry about 1 Bq Sr per g Ca
- Lets you predict a radioelement's behavior through the food chain from what is known about its congener

Related: [[Congener]], [[Sr-90]], [[Concentration Ratio]]
