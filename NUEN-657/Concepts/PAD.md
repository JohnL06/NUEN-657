#concept #protectiveaction
Protective Action Decision — the decision the decision maker actually makes to protect the public, usually based on a [[PAG]].

- The PAG is the guideline; the PAD is what gets ordered ([[evacuation]], [[shelter in place]], [[relocation]], [[Potassium Iodide]])
- Responders advise; the [[Incident Commander]] / decision maker decides

Related: [[PAG]], [[Incident Commander]], [[EPA400]]
