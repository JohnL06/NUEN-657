#concept #guidance
Protective Action Guide — "the projected dose to an individual, resulting from a radiological incident at which a specific protective action to reduce or avoid that dose is warranted." (EPA-400 glossary)

- Guidelines, not laws — leaves the decision maker room to think
- Based on projected dose (external groundshine/plume submersion + committed effective dose from inhalation)
- The decision actually made from a PAG is the [[PAD]]; the field-measurable stand-ins are the [[DRL]] (environment) and [[DIL]] (food)

| Phase | Action | PAG |
|---|---|---|
| Early | [[shelter in place]] or [[evacuation]] | 1-5 rem projected over 4 days (start at 1 rem) |
| Early | [[Potassium Iodide]] | 5 rem projected child thyroid dose |
| Intermediate | [[relocation]] | >2 rem first year; 0.5 rem/yr after |
| Intermediate | simple dose reduction | <2 rem first year |
| Intermediate | food interdiction | 0.5 rem/yr whole body or 5 rem/yr any organ |
| Intermediate | drinking water | 100 mrem/yr sensitive pops; 500 mrem/yr general |
| Late | cleanup | no PAG — planning guidance only |
