#concept #role
Public Information Officer — the ICS Command Staff position responsible for interfacing with the public and the media, so the response speaks with one voice.

- The "1 voice" principle: [[TMI]] had five federal agencies with five separate speakers to the public; that is now one PIO
- A member of the Command Staff, reporting to the [[Incident Commander]] or [[Unified Command]]

Related: [[Incident Commander]], [[ICS]], [[TMI]], [[FRMAC]]
