#concept #datasource
Personal Radiation Detectors — small, often pager-sized instruments worn by personnel that alarm automatically when they detect elevated radiation.

- Give nuclear/radiological responders a built-in early-warning capability that most other hazard types don't have — "nuke is nice because we have alarming PRDs"
- Commonly used alongside other field instruments during a response (see [[Field Teams]])
