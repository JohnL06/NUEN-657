#concept #transport
Downward redistribution of surface radionuclides to lower soil levels through a combination of physical and chemical processes.

- Driven by animal movement, tilling/digging, and rain wash
- Moves contamination from the soil surface (1-3 mm) into the root zone, then on toward deeper soil together with [[Leaching]]
- The opposite directions: [[Resuspension]] (surface into air) and heaving (root zone back up to the surface, e.g. hoeing or cracks in dry soil)

Related: [[Leaching]], [[Soil horizons]], [[Kd]], [[Terrestrial Dispersion]]
