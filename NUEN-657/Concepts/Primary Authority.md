#concept #role
Term used in the [[NRIA]] to describe the federal agency (or agencies) that own, have custody of, authorize, regulate, or are otherwise assigned responsibility for the nuclear/radiological material, facility, or activity involved in an incident.

- The agency holding Primary Authority is generally designated the [[LFA]] for that incident
- Distinct from a Cooperating Agency, which provides additional technical/resource support but does not itself hold primary authority

Related: [[LFA]], [[NRIA]]
