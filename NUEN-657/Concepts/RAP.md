#concept #asset
Radiological Assistance Program — [[DOE]]'s regionally based teams, usually the first federal radiological asset to arrive.

- Regional teams staffed from DOE national laboratories and sites
- Provide field monitoring, source search and identification, and advice to [[SLTT]] responders
- One of the [[NEST]] programs
- Can deploy under their own authority without calling D.C. — immediately deployable at field discretion
- The next to show up after [[CST]]; on scene in less than 8 hours after notification
- Support ranges from technical information or advice over the telephone, to field support at accident sites to help identify and minimize radiological hazards

Team of 6-8:
- Federal Team Lead — buffer between the team and local PD, has overall authority
- Captain — directs how things are done in the field
- Remainder are health physics personnel

Related: [[NEST]], [[DOE]], [[CMRT]], [[CST]], [[Field Teams]]
