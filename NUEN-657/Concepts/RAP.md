Radiological Assistance Program — [[DOE]]'s regionally based teams, usually the first federal radiological asset to arrive.

- Regional teams staffed from DOE national laboratories and sites
- Provide field monitoring, source search and identification, and advice to [[SLTT]] responders
- One of the [[NEST]] programs
