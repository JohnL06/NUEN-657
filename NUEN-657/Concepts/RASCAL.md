#concept #datasource
RASCAL (Radiological Assessment System for Consequence AnaLysis) — an NRC-developed software package for rapid dose-assessment and atmospheric dispersion modeling during a radiological emergency.

- Comes preloaded with data for every commercial power plant location in the US
- Allows adjustment of reactor burnup to refine the assumed [[Source term]]
- Used for quick, simplified atmospheric simulations rather than a full-detail transport model
