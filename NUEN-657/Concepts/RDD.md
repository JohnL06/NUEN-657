#concept #incident
Radiological dispersal device — a device that spreads radioactive material without a nuclear yield (a "dirty bomb").

- Classed by the [[NRIA]] among releases from deliberate acts, alongside improvised nuclear devices and nuclear weapons
- Likely sources named in lecture: Am-241, [[Sr-90]], Pu, Ir-192, [[Cs-137]]
- These are common industrial, medical, and research sources; loss, theft, or mismanagement of small sources is the most common N/R incident

Related: [[NRIA]], [[Cs-137]], [[Sr-90]]
