REAC/TS — Radiation Emergency Assistance Center / Training Site, [[DOE]]'s medical asset at Oak Ridge.

- Provides 24/7 consultation and treatment advice for radiation injury, contamination, and overexposure cases
- Also trains medical and health physics personnel in handling radiation casualties
- One of the [[NEST]] programs
