#concept #asset
Radiological Emergency Response Team — [[EPA]]'s deployable radiological asset, part of its National Decontamination Team.

- Provides monitoring, sampling, laboratory analysis, risk assessment, and cleanup support
- Becomes central once [[FRMAC]] transitions from [[DOE]] to EPA for long-term monitoring
- Specialized unit that responds to emergencies requiring the cleanup of radioactive substances, providing onsite and lab-based radiation risk monitoring services
- Draws on [[RADNET]] for fixed and mobile detection
- One of several EPA specialized teams, alongside [[CBRN CMAD]] and its [[ASPECT]] aircraft

Related: [[EPA]], [[RADNET]], [[CBRN CMAD]], [[FRMAC]], [[DOE]]
