#concept #datasource
The national platform responders use to collect and share radiological measurement data — the main way to send data to the [[FRMAC]].

- Alternatives if it is unavailable: email or fax to the [[CMHT]], or call data in on the CMHT bridge line
- Sponsored by FEMA, DOE/NNSA, and EPA; the course will hear from FEMA on it
- The data-sharing counterpart to [[CBRN Responder]]

Related: [[FRMAC]], [[CMHT]], [[CBRN Responder]], [[Field Teams]]
