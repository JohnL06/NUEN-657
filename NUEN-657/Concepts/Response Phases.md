#concept #policy
The three phases [[EPA400]] divides a radiological incident into.

- Early (emergency) phase — hours to days
	- PAGs may be based on default procedures (e.g. evacuate 2 miles in all directions on a site area emergency)
	- pathways: inhalation, deposition, direct exposure from the plume
	- actions: [[evacuation]], [[shelter in place]], [[Potassium Iodide]]
- Intermediate phase — weeks to months
	- source and releases (usually) under control; environmental measurements available
	- pathways: groundshine (dominant), inhalation from [[Resuspension]], ingestion
	- actions: [[relocation]], decon, shielding, time limits, food and water controls, reentry
	- [[Fukushima]] was a "never-ending early phase" — intermediate was roughly a month in
- Late phase — months to years
	- long-term cleanup; PAGs are NOT used to guide restoration
	- [[FRMAC]] monitoring hands over to [[EPA]]
