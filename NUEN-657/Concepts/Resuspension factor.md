#concept #transport
The ratio of airborne activity concentration to surface contamination — how much of what is on the ground is in the air above it.

- F_r (1/m) = X (Bq/m3) / S (Bq/m2)
- Rearranged: X = F_r · S, so multiply the measured deposition by F_r to get an air concentration
- Air samplers are deployed to measure resuspension so F_r can be determined for the site
- Estimates based on F_r have limitations and are not exact values
