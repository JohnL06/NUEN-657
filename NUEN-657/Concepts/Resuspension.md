 #data #concept 
The re-entrainment of previously deposited radioactive material back into the air — e.g., from wind, vehicle traffic, or agricultural activity — after it has already settled out of the original plume.

- Creates an ongoing, secondary inhalation hazard well after the plume itself has passed
- Relevant to long-term environmental monitoring and to re-entry/relocation decisions in a contaminated area
