#concept #agency
State, Local, Tribal, and Territorial governments — the standard federal shorthand for all non-federal governmental response partners.

- The owner/operator of an N/R facility notifies SLTT governments first, alongside the relevant federal D/As
- [[DOE]] cannot hand [[FRMAC]] to [[EPA]] until an initial long-range monitoring plan has been developed with SLTT and other federal agencies
- Radiological monitoring and assessment activities terminate in part when SLTT capabilities are adequate for the situation

Related: [[NRIA]], [[FRMAC]], [[Stafford Act]]
