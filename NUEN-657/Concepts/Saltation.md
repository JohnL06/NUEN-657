#concept #transport
Surface transport of medium particles (roughly 50-100 μm) that are lifted by wind, hop a short distance, and fall back down.

- Sits between [[Resuspension]] (< 50 μm, carried up into the air) and [[Surface creep]] (> 100 μm, rolled along the ground)

Related: [[Resuspension]], [[Surface creep]], [[Mass loading]]
