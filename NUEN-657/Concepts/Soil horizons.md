#concept #soil
The layered profile of a soil, broken into three major horizons.

- Top soil — first 30-60 cm, where most life processes take place
- Sub soil — down to about 1 m
- Parent material — around 1.5 m, loose and partly decayed rock
- Soil overall is minerals, organic matter, water, and air in a complicated physio-chemical system; it gives plants a foothold and supplies their nutrients
- For transport the lecture splits the profile functionally instead: soil surface (1-3 mm), root zone, and deeper soil
