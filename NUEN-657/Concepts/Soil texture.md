#concept #soil
The textural classes of the inorganic portion of soil, set by particle size.

- Sand — 60 μm to 2 mm, primary minerals such as quartz
- Silt — 2 to 60 μm
- Clay — smaller than 2 μm; plate-like aluminum silicates carrying an abundance of negative surface charge
- The clay fraction controls most of the physio-chemical processes that nourish plants, because it attracts and holds positive ions at its surface (see [[Ion Exchange]])
- Fraction sorbed is typically inversely proportional to particle size, so fine-textured soils hold contamination better
