 #data #concept 
The quantity, composition, timing, and physical/chemical form of radioactive material released from a facility or event.

- One of the first pieces of data a responding scientist needs, along with wind direction, weather, and rain
- Feeds directly into atmospheric transport simulations (see [[Weather Systems]]) to predict downwind [[Air concentration]] and [[Deposition amount]]
- Detecting alpha emitters in [[Field Teams]] samples is a strong indicator that fuel itself is being released/burned, which changes the assumed source term

Related: [[Weather Systems]], [[Air concentration]], [[Deposition amount]]
