#concept #nuclide
Strontium-90 — fission product with a roughly 29 year half-life (28.5 yr in lecture); one of the three most studied radionuclides because it drives a large share of dose from mixed fission product releases.

- [[Congener]] is calcium (same column of the periodic table), so it is a bone seeker
- Metabolism is similar to, but not identical with, calcium
- Tops the [[Concentration Ratio]] ordering (Sr >> I >> Ba > Cs)
- How much reaches people depends on diet — share of intake from milk: Chile 8%, US 77%, Finland 87%; cereals, legumes and vegetables dominate in the East
- A candidate [[RDD]] source

Related: [[Congener]], [[Observed Ratio]], [[Concentration Ratio]], [[Cs-137]], [[I-131]], [[RDD]]
