#concept #policy
Robert T. Stafford Disaster Relief and Emergency Assistance Act (1988) — the law that lets the President issue a major disaster or emergency declaration, unlocking federal assistance to [[SLTT]] governments.

- Once a declaration is issued: a [[FCO]] is designated, a [[JFO]] is established, and all [[ESF]]s become available
- The same 1988 act authorized FEMA's Director to prepare the [[FRP]] and required all states to prepare individual Emergency Operations Plans
- A Stafford Act response is one of the outcomes a [[Unified Command]] determines during the federal support determination for an N/R incident

Related: [[FCO]], [[JFO]], [[ESF]], [[FRP]], [[FEMA]], [[NRIA]]
