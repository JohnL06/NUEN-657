#concept #transport
Surface transport of the largest particles (> 100 μm), which roll and slide along the ground rather than leaving it.

- The coarse end of the three modes, with [[Saltation]] (50-100 μm) and [[Resuspension]] (< 50 μm)
- Moves contamination laterally but does not by itself create an inhalation hazard

Related: [[Saltation]], [[Resuspension]]
