#concept #incident
Three Mile Island — the 1979 partial core meltdown at Unit 2 near Harrisburg, Pennsylvania, and the origin of much of the current federal response structure.

- The disorganization of the response drove creation of the [[FRMAC]]
- Five federal agencies produced five speakers to the public; that is now a single [[PIO]]
- Five separate operations centers; that is now a single [[JOC]]

Related: [[FRMAC]], [[PIO]], [[JOC]], [[NRC]]
