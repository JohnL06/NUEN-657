#concept #transport
How radionuclides deposited on land move through and between soil, plants, animals, and air after a release.

- Rides the same energy flow and nutrient cycle as everything else: the sun (~2 cal/cm2·min) powers producers (plants), which feed primary and secondary consumers, while decomposers break down dead material and return nutrients to the pool producers draw from
- Because radionuclides follow nutrient pathways, ecosystem structure controls where contamination ends up
- Main mechanisms: [[Ion Exchange]] and [[Kd]] in soil, [[Percolation]] and [[Leaching]] downward, [[Resuspension]] upward, [[Foliar Deposition]] onto plants, [[Weathering]] from plants to soil, and uptake described by the [[Concentration Ratio]]

Related: [[Deposition amount]], [[Soil horizons]], [[Soil texture]], [[Kd]], [[Resuspension]]
