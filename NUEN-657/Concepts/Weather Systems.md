 #datasource #concept 
Meteorological data and forecasting used to predict plume direction, dispersion, and precipitation (which in turn drives deposition).

- National simulation groups draw on roughly 2,500 surface weather stations plus numerical weather prediction models
- Atmospheric transport simulations typically begin running 15–20 minutes after an event is reported in the US, with first results available in 30–60 minutes
- Output feeds directly into projected [[Air concentration]] and [[Deposition amount]]
