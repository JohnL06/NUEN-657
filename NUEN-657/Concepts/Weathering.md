#concept #transport
The process by which contamination on plant surfaces is transferred to the soil surface — superficial contamination removed mechanically.

- Exponential loss: (veg) = (veg)0 · e^(-k_w t)
- k_w = ln 2 / T_w, where T_w is the weathering half-time
- T_w ≈ 14 days for particles ≤ 20 μm; for semi-volatile [[I-131]], T_w ≈ 10 days
- For short-lived radionuclides, radioactive decay adds to weathering: (veg) = (veg)0 · e^(-(k_w + λ) t), with λ the decay constant
