Dose limits applied specifically during a radiological emergency response, distinct from the standard 5 rem/year occupational dose limit used in routine operations.

- Used to keep public and emergency worker doses below the thresholds for deterministic health effects (deaths and injuries) during the acute response phase
- The first objective of radiological emergency response; only after this is satisfied does the focus shift to reducing stochastic risk (cancer, hereditary effects) per [[EPA400]] and the [[FRMAC manual]]

Related: [[EPA400]], [[FRMAC manual]]
