#concept #protectiveaction
"The organized, phased, and supervised withdrawal, dispersal, or removal of people from dangerous or potentially dangerous areas, and their reception and care in safe areas." (IS-100.c glossary)

- One of the three protective actions available to a decision maker during a radiological release, alongside [[relocation]] and [[shelter in place]]
- Generally the action taken before or during passage of a plume, moving people out of the affected area entirely
- Decisions on when to call for evacuation vs. shelter-in-place are guided by Protective Action Guides (see [[EPA400]]) — guidance, not binding law, which is why responders can only point to "the guidelines" rather than dictate the decision

Related: [[Incident Commander]], [[relocation]], [[shelter in place]]
