#concept #protectiveaction
The longer-term protective action of moving people who have already been affected by contamination away from an area, as opposed to moving them out ahead of/during an acute exposure.

- Distinguished from [[evacuation]]: evacuation is short-term movement before or during passage of a plume; relocation applies afterward, based on measured or projected ground contamination, and can last weeks to years depending on decontamination progress
- Triggered by dose-based thresholds in the Protective Action Guides (see [[EPA400]])
- A long-term decision-maker question raised in lecture: how long must the population stay relocated, and is commerce in the area affected?
- PAG: >2 rem projected in the first year; 0.5 rem/year in the second and later years (the old 5 rem / 50 year PAG was removed)
- Areas defined by environmental factors — the "keyhole": a full circle around the site plus a downwind wedge to 5/10/25 miles
- Creates 3 groups: already evacuated; not evacuated but should be; evacuated but outside the relocation area, so may return
