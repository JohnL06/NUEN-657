#concept #protectiveaction
The protective action of remaining indoors — ideally in an interior or below-grade room — rather than evacuating through a moving plume.

- Often the preferred short-term action when a plume is moving quickly, when the release is expected to be brief, or when evacuation routes would themselves increase exposure
- One of the three main protective actions, alongside [[evacuation]] and [[relocation]]
- Decision governed by the same Protective Action Guide framework (see [[EPA400]]) used for the other protective actions
- PAG: 1-5 rem projected over 4 days (same trigger as evacuation); start at 1 rem
- Can be implemented rapidly; comes with instructions — HVAC off, seal gaps, go to the most interior room
- Dose reduction factors (lecture / EPA-400 Fig. 2-1): 1-story home ~2-3x, basement ~10x, large multi-story buildings 100-200x; go to lower or middle floors; a car helps only slightly
- Often better than evacuation when the plume is passing or responders can't support an evacuation

Related: [[evacuation]], [[relocation]], [[Incident Commander]], [[PAG]]
