[[FIOP]]’s Nuclear/Radiological Incident Annex (NRIA)
	- NERA (nuclear radiological) Annex
		- Fed guidance specific to the unique considerations of nera incidents 
		- nuke is nice because we have alarming [[PRDs]] 
		- administrative limits (guideline?) set:
			- 10 rem protection of property 
			- dose rate for emergency 96 hrs: 10 rem over 48 hours (12 hour workdays)
	- Focusses on activities that 
		- protect public
			- health
			- safety
			- property
			- critical infrastructure
			- environment
		- Restore gov services
		- provide emergency relief to
			- gov
			- businesses
			- individuals affected by the event
Purpose of NRIA
	- Defines roles and resopnsibibilities of federal agencies responding to nera events
	- discuss specific
	- authorities, capabilities, and assets that the fed gov has for responding to nera incidents (not specified in [[NRF]])
	- discuss integration of the CONOP (Concepts of Operations) with other elements of the NRF
	- provide guidelines for notification, coordination, and leadership of federal activities
A few terms
	- Lead Federal Agency ([[LFA]]) with [[Primary Authority]]
		- responsible for leading and coordinating all aspects of the federal response
		- FA that owns, authorizes, regulates, or deemed responsible for the facility, or activity involved in an incident.
	- D/As = Department / Agencies
	- SLTT = State, local, tribal and territorial
