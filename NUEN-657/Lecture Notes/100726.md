#Lecture
Vegetation to soil
	- [[Weathering]] is a process in which contamination on plants is transferred to the soil surface
		- Superficial contamination
		- Mechanical
			- $(\text{veg}) = (\text{veg})_0 e^{-k_w t}$
			- $k_w$ = weathering rate constant = $\dfrac{ln 2}{T_w}$
			- $T_w$ is weathering time
			- $T_w$ 14 days for particles <= 20 $\mu$m
			- [[I-131]] semi volatile $T_w$ = 10 days
			- for shortlived radionuclides
				- $(\text{veg}) = (\text{veg})_0 e^{-k_w + \lambda t}$
				- $\lambda$ is the normal decay constant

[[Foliar Deposition]]
	- Radioactive substances can contaminate plants directly
		- can pass directly to grazing animals or humans by ingestion
		- can be absorbed metabolically from the plant surface
	- High $K_d$ radionuclides or are high discriminated against by plants = soil ingestion dominates
	- for short lived radionuclides soil pathway is unimportant
	- growing seasons
		- Harvest
			- direct contamination is primary concern in harvest season, lowest in winter. direct deposition of old stems in surface roots may cause the radionuclides to be stored until spring when they are absorbed
				- structure of plants important, wheat can entrap fallout particles, cereals subject to high foliar concentrations
Food Chain
	- it is sometimes possible to predict the behavior of a radio element from the knowledge of its [[Congener|congeners]]
	- [[Observed Ratio]]
		- $OR_{\text{sample-precursor}}$ = $\dfrac{(\dfrac{Ce}{cc})_{\text{sample}}}{(\dfrac{Ce}{Cc})_{\text{prec}}}$
		- Ex: Ca-Sr OR = 0.1 if there is 10 $Bq \dfrac{Sr}{g C}$ then () in milk would be 1 $Bq \dfrac{Sr}{g C}$
Most studied Radionuclides
	- [[Sr-90]] Cs-137 and I-131 have been studied the most since they usually result in the highest doses to humans from the release of mixed fission products
	- Sr-90 Congener is Ca
		- Metabolism for Sr is NOT identical to Ca but similar
		- Tests have been done with Sr uniformly mixed with calcium
		- so amount in body depends if you get from food vs milk
			- Chile 8% from milk
			- US 77%
			- Finland 87%
			- cereal, legumes + veg in the east
Cs-137
	- Cs is bound by clays in soil
	- Cs is congener K
	- Cs:K is not constant in bio systems as Sr:Ca
		- 80% deposited in muscle
	- [[Effective half-life|Effective half life]] is a function of
		- Age
		- Sex
		- Dietary habits
	- infants: 19 +- 8 days
	- 105 days for men, women have more rapid turnover than men
	- dietary habits
		- Laplanders 50x more Cs than Finns due to reindeer consumption
Radioiodine
	- I is essential for normal thyroid function
	- 8 physical day half life
		- uptake from soil not a concern
	- Iodine deposited on plants eaten by cows released in milk (grass-cow-infant pathway)
	- Fraction of I that reaches thyroid is 0.3 with 7.6 day effective half life
	- take [[Potassium Iodide|stable I]] as long as 2 hours after exposure reduces the dose
