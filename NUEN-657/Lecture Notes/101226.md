Dose in General
- Biological damage from radiation
	- Types ($\alpha$ $\beta$ $\gamma$ n)
	- Where it is coming from
	- Energy
	- How it interacts with matter
- Radiation dosimetry is a branch of science that attempts to quantitatively relate specific measurements made in a radiation field to chem/bio changes the radiation produces in a target
- dose can be thought as energy imparted to target
	- in general, how is energy imparted to a target?
		- Photons: 
			- Photo Electric effect, Compton,  pair production
			- transfers energy to an electron (e-), which slows down and imparts energy
		- Neutrons:
			- Atomic interactions
- dose happens in 2 steps
	- interaction with photon causing electrons to be set in motion
	- transfer of energy by primary e- by the process of ionization or excitation
Exposure
- Defined Roentgen as the amount of energy required to liberate 2.58e-4 C/kg in air at STP (photons and air only)
- SI unit: "X" = 1 C/kg air @ stp, 3876 Roentgen

Absorbed Dose
- Charge accumulation in air is not helpful
- •Radiation deposits E in a medium
	- this deposition of E causes chemical and biological changes
	- Useful to know Energy imparted per unit mass
- Absorbed Dose = E abs per unit mass from any kind of ionizing radiation in any kind of material
- 1 R  = 2.88 10e-4 C/kg * \dfrac{ip}{1.6e-14 C} * 34 eV/ip * 1.6e-19 J/eV = 8.73 e-3 J/kg = 8.73e-3 Gy = 8.73 rad

Other types of Dose you may come across
- Effective dose accounts for the sensitivity of organ exposed
- Effective Dose Equivalent (EDE) accounts for the sensitivity of the organ AND the sensitivity to the  specific radiation
- Committed dose is dose due to an intake (consumption, breathing, injection)
	- CED committed effective dose
	- CEDE committed effective dose equivalent
- Total Effective Dose Equivalent (TEDE)
	- Accounts for external and internal doses (effective dose, equivalent, and committed)
