		- influenced by ground features

Gausian Plume Equation
	- $\chi (x,y) = \dfrac{Q}{\pi \sigma_y \sigma_z \bar u} \cdot e^{\dfrac{h^2}{2\sigma^2_z}+\dfrac{y^2}{2\sigma^2_y}}$
	- X is concentration (Bq or Ci/m3) near ground level at (x,y)
	- Q= source strength (Bq or Ci/s)
	- Sigma crosswind and vertical plume std dev (m), both are fcn of x
	- u = mean wind speed (m/s) at the stack elevation
	- h is effective stack height ($h_s$ + $\Delta$h, the plume rise)
	- for flat terrain these predictions are valid by a factor of 2-3 up to 10 km
